Variants (HTML)

Shows variants of markup based on the style, variation and locale. Markup is available for the following combinations:

Note that Wickes uses the following order for style, variation and locale: <base name>[_variation][_style][_<language code>[_<COUNTRY_CODE>[_<variant code>]]] in the filename, but that the priority is: style, variation, locale. That is why the DSL uses the latter order in its API.
